Perpetual Grid Bot

A grid places buy and sell orders at fixed price steps and books a small gain each time the price swings through them. This simulator replays a synthetic BTC price path and shows fills, fees, funding and liquidation risk.

Simulated prices and fixed demo fees. No API keys, no exchange connection, no real orders. Not investment advice.
