At Arkham I manage the CIS community. A large part of the job is not trading. It is collecting what active users keep asking for and passing it to the product team.

## What users kept asking for

After the platform moved from labeling centralized exchanges toward more DEX coverage, the requests I relayed were about the CEX to DEX transition, VIP benefits and reproducible bugs.

## What I did with it

I grouped the requests, added examples users had flagged and sent them to the product team. These requests fed into the CEX to DEX transition, VIP benefits and bug fixes.

## What this does not show

It does not show any price impact, and I do not claim one. It shows that I can run a feedback loop between users and a product team.
