While leading two CIS communities for Arkham, most of my time was not trading. It was synthesizing what active users
kept asking for and turning that into a short brief for the product team.

## What we heard

A recurring theme after the platform shifted from pure CEX labeling toward more DEX coverage: users wanted clearer
flows between exchange deposits and on-chain destinations, better VIP-tier clarity, and faster fixes for labeling
bugs they could reproduce.

## What we did with it

I packaged examples (screenshots, wallet links, short write-ups) and sent them upstream. Several items moved from
"nice to have" to sprint work: DEX transition tooling, VIP benefit copy, and bug-fix batches tied to specific
addresses users flagged.

## Outcome (long)

The win was organizational: community signal → spec → shipped fixes. I do not claim price impact from this work. It
is evidence I can run a feedback loop between users and builders, which is the same muscle I use in token research
before writing tools like the portfolio projects here.

## Relation to this site

None of the MVPs on this site were Arkham features. The pattern (listen → document → ship) is the same one I use when
I build Funding Rate Radar or Wallet Cluster Investigator for hiring managers who want to see process, not hype.
