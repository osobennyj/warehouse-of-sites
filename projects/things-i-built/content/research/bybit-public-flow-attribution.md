Wallet Cluster Investigator shipped with three committed investigations around a **public** February 2025 fund-flow
story that analysts already discussed on social channels. My goal was not to break news or call a top.

## Research question

Given a seed address, can I expand funding relationships with Blockscout data, apply four transparent heuristics, and
publish JSON + Markdown a reviewer can rerun?

## Outcome (other realized)

The realized outcome was **deliverable quality for hiring**: open viewer, open evidence panel, exportable
investigation files. Clusters are scored with reasons (common funder, synchronized activity, sweep patterns). The UI
copy states attribution is probabilistic.

## What this is not

No claim of insider knowledge, no implied PnL, no timeline to "when price moved." The project demonstrates
methodology I also used in Arkham community work, but as my own open toolchain.

## Tooling

Built with **Wallet Cluster Investigator** (live MVP at `/demos/wallet-cluster-investigator/`). Not related to AI Token
Due Diligence or Funding Rate Radar except as sibling portfolio proofs.
